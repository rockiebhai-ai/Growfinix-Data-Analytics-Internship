# Task 5: Final Sales Dashboard & Business Insights Report
- **Tools Used:** Google Sheets, Interactive Slicers, KPI Cards, Charts
## Top 5 Actionable Business Insights
1. **High-Ticket Electronics Drive Revenue:** Laptops account for over 83% of total 
revenue (₹1,10,000 out of ₹1,32,400), making them the primary revenue driver.
2. **Accessories Drive Order Volume:** Mice and Keyboards generate the highest unit sales 
volume (9 units combined), making them ideal for bundle discounts.
3. **Top Performing Region:** Chennai leads in total order frequency (37.5% of all 
orders), followed by Delhi.
4. **Data Quality Impact:** Standardizing city names and removing duplicate entries 
prevented a 12% over-reporting error in initial sales figures.
5. **Growth Recommendation:** Introduce combo offers pairing Laptops with Wireless Mice/
Keyboards in Chennai and Bangalore to increase average order value.
